# Short-form policy drafts

## 1. AI Transparency Disclosure (Style Concierge)

The Style Concierge is an AI assistant, not a human stylist. It suggests outfits, items, brands, and advisors based on what you tell it. Its suggestions are general style ideas, not professional advice, and they may be wrong or unsuitable for you. It can't see your body, wardrobe, or budget beyond what you type. It doesn't know about stock or prices, and it isn't paid to recommend any brand. Advisor recommendations come only from advisors listed on Cook A Look, and labeled sample profiles are not real advisors. For personal advice, book a session with an advisor. To report a harmful or inappropriate answer, email info@cookalook.com *(add an in-app report link — recommended)*.

## 2. Cookie & Browser Storage Notice

Cook A Look doesn't use advertising or analytics cookies, tracking pixels, or session-recording tools.

We use your browser's storage only for things the site needs to work:
- **Sign-in session** (local storage, set by our authentication provider) keeps you signed in.
- **Style Concierge conversation** (session storage) keeps your chat while you browse. It's cleared when you close the tab.

Third parties that load content on our pages (Google Fonts, Google Maps on the booking form, Stripe on its checkout page, and Daily.co during video calls) receive your IP address and may set their own cookies under their own policies. Because we use no non-essential trackers, no consent banner is shown. If we add analytics or marketing tools, we'll ask for consent first where required. *(Lawyer to confirm.)*

## 3. In-Person Session Safety Guidance

- **Meet in public.** Advisors propose public venues such as stores, cafés, or studios. Don't share your home address or meet at a private residence for a first session.
- **Keep communication on Cook A Look** until the session, so there's a record if something goes wrong.
- **Tell someone** where you're going and when.
- **Verification has limits.** Advisors confirm their identity with a government ID and a live photo, and must be 18 or older. Cook A Look does **not** run criminal background checks.
- **You can leave** at any time if you feel uncomfortable.
- **Report problems** within 48 hours from your dashboard, or to info@cookalook.com. In an emergency, call 911.

We may suspend accounts while we review a report.

## 4. Intellectual Property Complaint Procedure

If you believe content on Cook A Look (such as a profile photo, portfolio image, or bio) infringes your copyright or other rights, email **info@cookalook.com** with:
1. your name and contact details;
2. a description of the work and your rights in it;
3. the URL of the content;
4. a statement that you believe in good faith the use isn't authorized; and
5. a statement that your notice is accurate.

We'll review the notice, may remove or disable the content, and will tell the person who posted it. They can respond if they believe it was removed by mistake. Repeat infringers may be removed.

*Lawyer to assess:* Canada's notice-and-notice regime (Copyright Act) and whether to register a DMCA designated agent with the US Copyright Office if serving US users. Don't claim DMCA safe harbour until that's decided.

**Content ownership (for the Terms):** Advisors keep ownership of the photos and content they upload and grant Cook A Look a licence to display them on the platform and in promotion of their profile. Advisors must have the rights to everything they upload, including photos of other people.

## 5. Acceptable Use Policy

When using Cook A Look, you must not:
- harass, threaten, discriminate against, or demean anyone;
- impersonate anyone or misrepresent your identity, qualifications, or affiliations;
- arrange payment outside Cook A Look for sessions found through it;
- post content you don't have the rights to, or sexual, violent, or illegal content;
- upload malware or try to access accounts, data, or systems you're not authorized to use;
- post fake or incentivized reviews, or manipulate ratings or rewards (including self-referrals);
- spam, scrape, or misuse the Style Concierge;
- behave unsafely during in-person sessions.

**Reporting:** email info@cookalook.com, or use the dispute tools in your dashboard. **Enforcement:** we may remove content, issue warnings, hold payouts during an investigation (as described in the Terms), suspend, or close accounts, depending on how serious the conduct is. We'll tell you why, unless doing so would put someone at risk or break the law.
