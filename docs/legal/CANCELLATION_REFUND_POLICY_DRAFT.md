## Cancelling a session (clients)

You can cancel any upcoming session from your dashboard. Before you confirm, we show you the exact refund amount.

| When you cancel | Video session | In-person session |
|---|---|---|
| Any time before the late-cancellation window | Full refund | Full refund |
| Within 1 hour of a video session / 2 hours of an in-person session | 90% refund (10% late-cancellation fee) | 90% refund (10% late-cancellation fee) |
| After the start time | No refund | No refund |

Refunds go back to your original payment method through Stripe. They usually appear within 5 to 10 business days, depending on your bank.

## If your advisor cancels
You receive a **full refund**, whenever they cancel.

## If your advisor doesn't show up
Report it from your dashboard within 48 hours of the scheduled start time. Payments are held until 48 hours after the session, so we can review the report and refund you. *(Confirm the no-show process with the admin dispute workflow before publishing.)*

## Technical problems
If a video session can't take place because of a problem on our side, contact us within 48 hours and we'll review it for a full or partial refund.

## Disputes
You can open a dispute within 48 hours of the scheduled start time. Funds stay on hold while we review messages, booking records, and, for video sessions, the recording.
